About Gunita

What is Gunita?

"Gunita" is a Filipino word meaning memory or remembrance. It is a digital sanctuary for the words we couldn't say and the feelings we dare not forget. Here, you can pin your thoughts to a map, releasing them into the wind for 14 days before they fade away forever.

Our Mission

To provide a safe harbor for unspoken emotions. In a world where everything is permanent and public, Gunita offers the beauty of ephemerality. We believe that the act of releasing a thought—even if no one reads it—is the first step to healing.

The Philosophy of Fading

Why do memories fade after 14 days? Because real feelings are not meant to be archived forever like data. They are meant to be felt, experienced, and eventually let go. This impermanence makes every word you read here precious and alive.

The Keeper's Oath

Gunita is a sanctuary, not a battlefield. We ask all Keepers to respect the vulnerability of others. Hate speech, harassment, or malicious content has no place here. If you see something that breaks this peace, use the Report button. Let us protect this space together.
